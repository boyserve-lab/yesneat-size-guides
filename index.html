US home goods sizes in inches

Finished dimensions for bed frames, bedding, bath mats, rugs, pillows and storage, plus the clearances that decide whether something actually fits.

Spec tables list nominal sizes. A bed frame adds an allowance per side, a duvet insert is sized to the drop, and a bath mat has to clear a door swing. The numbers below are the finished sizes, not the size on the box.

Storage and closets

Cube storage is 11 or 13 inches. Cabinet depth is 12, 15, 18 or 24 inches. Closet depth is 24 inches, minus 3 inches for a hinged door and 4 inches for a sliding door. A 12 gallon tote is about 24 x 16 x 10 inches and a 27 gallon tote about 30 x 18 x 14 inches.

How to read a chart

Width is left to right across the bed, length is head to foot. A number in a chart is the finished size, not the size on the box. If a measurement lands within 1 inch of a limit, assume it does not fit.
